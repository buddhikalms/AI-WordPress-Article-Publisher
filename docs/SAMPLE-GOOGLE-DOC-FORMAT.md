# Sample Google Doc Format

Copy this structure into a Google Doc. Share the doc as `Anyone with the link can view`, then paste the document link into Google Doc Import.

```text
---
title: Best AI SEO Tools for Agencies
slug: best-ai-seo-tools-for-agencies
excerpt: A practical comparison of AI SEO tools for agency teams.
brief: Compare tools for agencies scaling keyword research, outlines, and publishing workflows.
categories: SEO, AI Tools
tags: ai seo, agency workflows, content automation
focus_keyword: ai seo tools for agencies
canonical_url:
featured_image_url:
---

# Best AI SEO Tools for Agencies

Place your featured image directly below the title if you do not use `featured_image_url`.
The importer uses the first image in the Google Doc as the WordPress featured image.

## Introduction

Write the article body here.

## Key Takeaways

- Point one
- Point two
- Point three

## Tool Comparison

Add your prepared article sections, links, and supporting images.

## FAQ

### What is the best AI SEO tool for agencies?

Answer the question in a concise paragraph.
```

SEO notes:

- Leave `seo_title` and `meta_description` out when you want AI Article Publisher to generate AIOSEO fields automatically.
- AI-generated SEO title is capped at 60 characters.
- AI-generated meta, Open Graph, and Twitter descriptions are capped at 155 characters.
- If `featured_image_url` is blank, the first image embedded in the doc is uploaded and set as the featured image.
